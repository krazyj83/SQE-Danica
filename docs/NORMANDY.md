# Flying Normandy 1944

1. **File > New Campaign**, pick the **Normandy 1944** theatre. The year switches to 1944 and only the Allied aircraft are offered: P-51D, P-47D, Spitfire IX and Mosquito. Pick one of the theatre's squadrons or raise a new one.
2. The tasking order has close support around Caen, flak suppression, strikes on fuel, depots and headquarters, counter-air sweeps over the German fields, and intercepts of Ju 88 raids on the landing grounds.
3. Run the **terrain scan** once (Settings > Create terrain scan mission). The built-in Channel coastline is coarse (a few km), so the scan matters more here than on the Caucasus.

**Radios.** WWII sets are VHF with a few crystal channels set in the mission. SQE puts ATC, the forward air controller and the divert field on VHF, but it cannot program warbird radio presets yet. Set them from the kneeboard comms page in the Mission Editor if you want to talk to them.

**Loadouts.** P-47 and P-51 bombs and rockets come from pydcs's pylon tables. The Spitfire carries British 250 and 500 lb bombs. The Mosquito has no pylon table in pydcs, so save Mission Editor presets named `SQE STRIKE` and `SQE CAS` for it; SQE then uses them.

**Not in yet:** standing patrols over the beachhead (the planner only flies BARCAP over a carrier), Allied heavy bombers, and Marianas WWII. pydcs has neither that map nor Japanese aircraft.
